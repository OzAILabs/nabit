Nabit privacy policy

Nabit Screen Capture, by OzAILabs. Last updated 16 November 2026.

In short: Nabit works on your PC. It does not collect your information, it has no account, and it does not send your captures, recordings or settings anywhere unless you choose to share them.

What Nabit handles

Nabit captures your screen, records it with the system sound, your microphone and (if you turn it on) your webcam, and lets you mark the result up. These can show personal information, such as names, messages or faces. All of it stays on your PC:

  • Captures, recordings and projects are saved in your own Library folder, or wherever you save them.
  • Your settings are saved on your PC.
  • Text recognition (Grab Text) runs on your PC. Nothing is uploaded for it.
  • The microphone and webcam are used only while you record with them switched on.

What Nabit sends

Nothing, unless you ask it to. When you use Share to send a capture by email, to an FTP server, to another program or to a chat, the capture goes to that place and is then handled under that service's own policy. Nabit has no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and it sends no usage or crash reports.

Nabit Pro and the Microsoft Store

Nabit is free. Nabit Pro is a one-time purchase made in the Microsoft Store. The Store handles the payment, and OzAILabs does not receive your card or payment details. Nabit only asks Windows and the Store whether your Microsoft account owns Nabit Pro. A licence key, if you use one, is checked on your PC.

Children

Nabit is a general-purpose tool and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact
